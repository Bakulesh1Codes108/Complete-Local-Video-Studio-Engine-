3. The Unified Multi-Engine Render Architecture

The studio unifies multiple industry-leading open-source technologies into one pipeline:

  • Remotion Core (React + TypeScript): Deterministic frame-accurate canvas for 4K Ken Burns, layout scaling, and multi-track audio ducking.
  • Open Higgsfield 3D Camera Rigs: Transforms static media and 3D scenes using physical camera physics (orbit_360, fpv_drone_dive, vertigo_dolly_zoom, whip_pan, crane_up_reveal, bullet_time_freeze).
  • Three.js 3D Procedural Scenes: Built-in holographic Earth globe (3d_rotating_globe), quantum particle waves (3d_quantum_particles), and animated financial bar charts (3d_financial_chart).
  • Blender Headless 3D Engine: Python CLI automation for photorealistic 3D chrome titles and product turntables without GUI overhead.
  • Manim Engine: 3Blue1Brown-style mathematical formula morphs and neural network animations.

4. Vertical V3 Architecture (9:16 Shorts & Reels)

Standard video renderers place subtitles at the bottom of the screen, where they are obscured by TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts UI overlays (sound title, captions, like/comment buttons).

Key Innovations of Vertical V3:
  • Platform Safe-Zone Positioning: Subtitles are automatically elevated (~32% from bottom) so they are 100% visible across all mobile apps.
  • Kinetic Word-by-Word Highlight: Words pop in real-time with vibrant #FFEE00 neon yellow highlight boxes, impact typography, and thick black strokes as they are spoken (Hormozi / MrBeast style).
  • Smart Dual-Layer Blur Pad: If 16:9 landscape assets are used, the engine automatically creates a blurred, saturated ambient background fill behind the sharp, centered foreground visual.
  • Top-Edge Retention Progress Bar: A smooth gradient progress bar tracks video completion across the top edge to maximize viewer watch-time.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the studio run on an 8GB RAM Mac without crashing?
The studio runs in Cloud-Assisted Mode: Gemini 2.0 Flash / OpenRouter handles story arc planning, Pixabay delivers stock visuals, Edge-TTS synthesizes neural narration, and Remotion renders frame-accurately on CPU with zero GPU memory overhead.
Q: How does Vertical V3 fix TikTok and Instagram subtitle obstruction?
Standard video editors pin subtitles at the screen bottom (where captions, sound titles, and like/comment buttons cover them). Vertical V3 elevates subtitles into the safe middle third (~32% from bottom) with kinetic active-word bouncing highlight boxes.
Q: How do I export for both YouTube (16:9) and TikTok (9:16) simultaneously?
Choose the omni format in the copilot, or run python3 scripts/studio.py convert 9:16 to convert between widescreen and vertical reels in one second.
